Good questions

Before we
grind.

How much does stump grinding cost?

Every quote depends on the stump and property. Size, root flare, quantity, access, terrain, nearby structures and desired cleanup can all affect the scope. Send photos for a free, no-hassle quote.

Do you handle one stump or multiple stumps?

Yes. Public company positioning covers work from a single stump to larger lot-clearing stump projects.

Do you serve homes and commercial properties?

Yes. GRIND IT serves both residential and commercial customers.

How should I measure my stump?

Measure straight across the widest part near ground level, including the visible root flare. An approximate measurement is useful; photos provide additional context.

What photos help with a quote?

Send one close photo showing the complete stump and one wider photo showing the route from the driveway or gate to the stump.

What access does the grinder need?

Access requirements depend on the equipment selected for your job. Measure gates or narrow points and share the width so the team can confirm feasibility.

What happens to the wood chips?

Grinding produces a mixture of wood chips and soil. Whether grindings are left, moved or hauled should be confirmed in your quote.

Will stump grinding damage my yard?

Professional planning considers access, terrain and the surrounding work area. Share slopes, soft ground, irrigation and nearby structures so the team can discuss the approach and any risks before work begins.

What areas do you serve?

GRIND IT is based in Walhalla and publicly serves Oconee County and surrounding areas. Send your location to confirm a specific property.

How soon can I schedule service?

Availability changes. Call, text or request a quote and the team can confirm the current schedule.
